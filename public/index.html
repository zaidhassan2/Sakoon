How this works

What this space is

Sukoon is a free, quiet companion for moments of everyday stress. You can talk through what is on your mind or use offline exercises at your own pace.

Your privacy

We do not ask for your name, email, or account. We do not use cookies or analytics. Your conversation stays only in this browser tab until you close it or clear it. When you send a message, the recent turns are sent to an AI language service so it can generate a reply.

Important limits

An AI cannot replace human care, medical advice, or crisis support. Whenever things feel too heavy, you can reach free, confidential human helplines with one tap.

About Sukoon

What this is

Sukoon is a calm companion designed for low-energy moments when stress builds up. Everything here is an invitation—you are always in control of what to share, skip, or clear.

What this is not

This is an AI tool, not a human counsellor or emergency service. It does not diagnose, judge, or keep records about you.

Privacy in plain words

Your chat lives only in temporary session memory in this tab. Exercises run entirely on your device and never send anything to a server. Tapping "Clear and leave" erases all temporary data and takes you to a neutral web page.
